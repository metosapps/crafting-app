About the game

Your blocky adventure awaits

Welcome to Crafting — a free sandbox in a blocky world where your imagination has no limits. Explore vast open worlds, gather resources, and build anything you can dream of, from a tiny hut to a massive castle.

By day, collect materials and build. By night, defend yourself and survive.

Game features

  • Endless procedurally-generated world — every game is unique
  • Survival mode — mine, craft and survive
  • Free building — hundreds of blocks and materials
  • Diverse biomes: forests, mountains, oceans, deserts
  • Friendly and dangerous creatures
  • Character customization
  • Smooth touch controls
  • Play offline — no internet required

Gather wood, stone and ore and turn them into tools, weapons and structures. Houses, castles, farms, entire cities — you are limited only by your imagination.
